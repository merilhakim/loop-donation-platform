Poverty

Poverty is more than not having enough money. It can mean not having reliable access to food, clean water, safe shelter, education, healthcare or stable employment.

According to the World Bank, more than 824 million people were living in extreme poverty in 2024.

Children are especially vulnerable. UNICEF reports that around 412 million children live in extreme monetary poverty, while nearly 900 million children experience multidimensional poverty.

The organizations below work in different ways to reduce poverty, support families, respond to crises and improve access to basic services and economic opportunities.
