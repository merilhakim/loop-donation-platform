About Us

Hello!

We are two young girls who met while learning programming at Loop Campus.

Our story began in 2018, when the Loop team invited us to participate in a programming competition. That is where we met and started working together on a project with one goal: to help connect people who want to make a difference with organizations that support people in need.

We created this website to help users discover organizations working in areas such as hunger, clean water, clothing, education and shelter.

Through this project, we learned how technology can be used to create awareness and connect people with opportunities to help others.

What started as a programming project became an experience that inspired us to keep learning, building and improving.
