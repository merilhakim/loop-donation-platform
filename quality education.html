Quality Education

Education gives children and young people the knowledge and skills they need to build opportunities for their future.

Quality education is not only about attending school. Students also need trained teachers, safe learning environments, books and learning materials, and equal opportunities to succeed.

Some children face additional barriers because of poverty, gender inequality, displacement, conflict or humanitarian emergencies.

The organizations below work to improve access to education, literacy and learning opportunities for children and young people.




ORGANIZATION FOCUS WHERE THEY WORK INFORMATION WEBSITE
Room to Read Literacy and girls' education International Room to Read helps children develop foundational literacy skills and supports gender equality through education programs. Save the Children Child education and early learning International Save the Children supports schools, teachers and communities to help children access safe and inclusive education. Its education programs include early learning, literacy and support for children whose education is disrupted by emergencies or crises. Visit Organization
Education Cannot Wait Education in emergencies Global Education Cannot Wait is the United Nations global fund for education in emergencies and protracted crises. It supports learning for children affected by conflict, displacement, disasters and other humanitarian emergencies. Visit Organization
UNICEF Children's education and basic services Global UNICEF works with governments and partners to improve children's access to education, especially for children affected by poverty, emergencies and inequality.
