Fighting Poverty Through Awareness and Action

Poverty means not having enough resources to meet basic needs such as food, clean water, clothing, education and shelter.

It can also mean not having access to healthcare, education, stable housing or opportunities for a secure future.

Our goal is to help people discover organizations that work to support communities in need and make it easier to find ways to help.
