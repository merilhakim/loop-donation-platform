Hunger

Hunger happens when people do not have enough food to meet their nutritional needs and live healthy, active lives.

According to the World Food Programme, around 645 million people experienced hunger in 2025.

In 2026, around 266 million people are facing acute hunger in countries affected by food crises.

Conflict, economic shocks and climate-related events are among the major factors that contribute to food insecurity.

The organizations below work to provide food, emergency assistance, nutrition programs and long-term solutions to hunger.
