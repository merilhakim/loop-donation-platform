Clean Water

Access to safe water and sanitation is essential for health, education and everyday life.

Water.org currently reports that around 2.1 billion people lack access to safe water and around 3.4 billion people lack access to a safe toilet.

Lack of clean water can affect health, education and economic opportunity, especially for women and children.

The organizations below work to improve access to safe drinking water, sanitation and hygiene.




ORGANIZATION FOCUS WHERE THEY WORK INFORMATION WEBSITE
Water.org Safe water and sanitation International Water.org is a global nonprofit that helps people gain lasting access to safe water and sanitation. Its work focuses on removing financial and practical barriers that prevent families from accessing safe water and toilets. Visit Organization
charity: water Clean drinking water projects International charity: water funds projects that provide reliable access to clean and safe drinking water. The organization highlights the connection between clean water, health, education, dignity and economic opportunity. Visit Organization
