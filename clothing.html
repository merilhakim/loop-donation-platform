Clothing

Access to suitable clothing is an important part of health, safety and dignity.

Clothing helps protect people from weather conditions, environmental hazards and exposure.

For children, families and people experiencing hardship, access to appropriate clothing can also affect education, employment and everyday participation in society.

The organizations below provide different ways to help, including donating clothing, shoes, coats or financial support.




ORGANIZATION FOCUS WHERE THEY WORK INFORMATION WEBSITE
Soles4Souls Shoes and clothing International Soles4Souls collects new and gently used shoes and clothing. The organization works to extend the life of donated items while supporting people in crisis, entrepreneurs and communities around the world. Visit Organization
One Warm Coat Coats and winter clothing United States One Warm Coat works to provide free coats to children and adults in need. Volunteers can donate coats or organize local coat drives, and nonprofit partners distribute them within their communities. Visit Organization
Dress for Success Professional clothing and employment support Global network Dress for Success helps women work toward economic independence. Its programs include professional attire, career support, job-readiness resources and workplace development. Visit Organization
UNICEF Children and humanitarian aid International UNICEF works to protect children's rights and support children and families affected by poverty, emergencies and humanitarian crises. Its work includes access to basic necessities, health, education and emergency support. Visit Organization
